What changed. The headline is the action in plain words, derived from the profile plus action type — not the tool name. The one line under it is the thing you'd actually want to check: who it went to, what was written, where it deployed. Machine identifiers move into a collapsed Technical block, and the profile version survives as a small badge because after today it tells you whether recipients were bound.

View receipt links to {spUrl}/r/{id} — the AS already publishes spUrl on /health, so nothing new is needed. content bound appears when the receipt carries a content hash, which is the difference between "this happened" and "this exact content was approved".

One thing I'd flag: "Email sent" for a create_draft call would be a lie. The plain-language label has to come from the manifest's action_type, not be guessed from the profile — otherwise this trades an ugly card for a misleading one.
