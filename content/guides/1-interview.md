# Interview

Learn the company, the team and the work, and collect real cases. Nothing is loaded or proposed in this step.

You ask; the person answers and checks. Ask one topic at a time and write down what you learn.

## Before you start: the systems

Look at the systems in the header above. If a system the work needs is listed under **Available but not activated**, ask the person to activate it first (Integrations → its card → Activate) and read this guide again. Do not plan around a system you cannot see.

Plan the test only with the actions the connected systems offer — the header lists each system's changes and reads. If the work needs an action no system offers (for example a refund when the ERP has only quotes and orders), say so and agree with the person on what to test instead. Never pretend a system can do more than it does.

## What to ask, in this order

1. **Company:** what it sells, to whom, in which currency, and which systems it uses. The systems connected to this gateway are listed in the header above; ask about the ones that matter for the work.
2. **Department and team:** who does the work today, what a normal day looks like, what must never happen.
3. **The work to test:** which requests come in (for example quote requests, orders, questions), how many, how fast they are answered today, and what a good answer looks like.
4. **Order of systems:** whether the team follows a fixed sequence across systems — for example: always look up the customer in the CRM before quoting in the ERP, and log the reply in the CRM after sending. Which step must come first, which may be skipped. This becomes the agent's process and a rule in its brief.
5. **Real cases:** 5–10 recent examples, each with the request as it arrived and the reply that was actually sent. The person pastes or forwards them.
6. **Rules that are not written down:** discounts, credit limits, who may promise delivery dates, when to escalate and to whom.

## Renaming

Before anything else happens, propose new names for every person, company and email domain in the cases. Keep amounts, products and dates realistic. Use `.example` domains (for example `einkauf@huber.example`). The person checks the renamed cases; change them until they agree.

## Result of this step

- A short written summary: company, team, the work, the order of systems, the rules.
- The renamed cases.

Both confirmed by the person. The summary feeds the agent brief and the mandates; the cases feed the test data.

## Ask, don't guess

If a rule or a step is unclear, ask the person. Never invent a rule, a limit or a step to fill a gap.
